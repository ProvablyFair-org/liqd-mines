# LIQD Mines — Evidence Register

**Audit ID:** `PF-2026-LQ02` · part of the [audit overview](MANIFEST.md). Every exhibit of the audit: what it is, where it came from and its SHA-256. The evidence is the data and the code: every cryptographic and payout claim is reproducible offline from E01–E04 and E15–E18, and every derived figure the report quotes is emitted into E05, E06 or E12. All exhibits come from production (`liqd.com`, 8–10 November 2026) or from this package's own runs on that data; E14 is the read of LIQD's public page, E23 a read of LIQD's verifier without a login, and E24 and E25 reads of LIQD's account bet history for the CAD and USD runs (all 11 November 2026). No screen capture is part of the evidence (limitation DL-19 in the overview).

The SHA-256 column is checked against the files by `scripts/check-standalone.mjs`, the first stage of `npm test`: a hash here that is not the file's fails the run.
